What every site holds

The same four building blocks, no matter which of the three site types you're standing in front of.

Equipment register

Every machine gets its own commissioning checklist, make, model, serial and service interval — turning due soon and overdue automatically.

Daily activity log

Work done, crew size, hours and issues — with up to six photos per entry, timestamped from the field.

Readings & flagging

The parameters that matter for that site type, logged over time — out-of-range values flag themselves in the next update.

Team & roles

Owners, site leads, technicians and senior officers — one join code brings a new person straight into the organisation.

Built for who's actually standing at the site
